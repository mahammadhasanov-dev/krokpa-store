Overview

KROKPA operates the KROKPA store to give you a straightforward shopping experience. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you visit or buy from us.

Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our site, we may collect: contact details (name, address, phone, email), order and payment details (card information is entered directly into our payment processor's secure fields and never touches our servers), account information if you create one, and general device and usage data.

Where We Get This Information

Directly from you at checkout or account signup, automatically as you browse via cookies and analytics, and from the service providers who help us run the store.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To send order updates and, where you've opted in, marketing emails
  • For fraud prevention and account security
  • To provide customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Sharing

We share information with the vendors who help us operate: our payment processor (Stripe) to securely process payments, our shipping partners to deliver orders, and analytics providers to understand site usage. We don't sell your personal information.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@example.com.

Children's Privacy

KROKPA is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@example.com or mailed to the address above.
