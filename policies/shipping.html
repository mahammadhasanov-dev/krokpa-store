Processing Time

Every cap is made to order. Orders placed before 2:00 PM PT begin production the same day; otherwise production starts the next business day. Standard production takes 1–3 business days before your order ships.

Delivery Timeframes

  • United States: 2–5 business days after production
  • Canada: 4–8 business days after production
  • International: 7–14 business days after production

Shipping Costs

Shipping is free on orders over 150 ₼. Orders under 150 ₼ ship for a flat ₼8.90 rate within Azerbaijan. Shipping outside Azerbaijan is calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight.

Customs & International Orders

International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes on arrival. These charges are set by your local customs authority and are the responsibility of the customer.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos and your order number and we'll sort out a replacement or refund.

Order Tracking

You'll get an order confirmation email at checkout, and a tracking link by email as soon as your cap ships.

Contact Us

Questions about a shipment? Email support@example.com — we typically respond within 1–2 business days.
